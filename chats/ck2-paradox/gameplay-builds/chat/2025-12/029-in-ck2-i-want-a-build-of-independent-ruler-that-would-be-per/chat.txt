This is a classic Crusader Kings 2 dilemma. You want a character who can survive the chaos and expand, but you don't want to start as an immortal demigod, because that robs the game of its tension.

Here is a build designed for **Stability and Economy**. As a newcomer, your biggest enemies will be running out of money and vassals hating you. This build solves both, allowing you to focus on learning the warfare and marriage mechanics.

### The Philosophy: "The Enlightened Administrator"
This build focuses on **Stewardship**. High stewardship gives you a larger Demesne Limit (you can hold more castles personally without penalties) and higher tax income. Holding more land personally means you have more troops than your vassals, preventing rebellions.

### 1. Education Trait
*   **Midas Touched (Stewardship Level 4)**
    *   *Effect:* Huge boost to Stewardship and Fertility.
    *   *Why:* Money is the "fix mistake" button in CK2. If you mess up a war, money buys mercenaries. If a vassal hates you, money buys a gift.

### 2. Base Attributes
(In the Ruler Designer, set these manually. We keep them low to save "points" for traits, which are more valuable).
*   **Diplomacy:** 3
*   **Martial:** 5
*   **Stewardship:** 6
*   **Intrigue:** 0
*   **Learning:** 0
    *   *Note:* Don't worry about the zeros. The traits below will boost these up to respectable levels.

### 3. Positive Traits (The "Min-Max")
These are expensive in terms of age/points, but essential.

*   **Genius**
    *   *Cost:* High
    *   *Effect:* +5 to **All** Attributes.
    *   *Why:* This is the only "OP" trait we are taking. It is crucial for a newcomer because it makes your heir likely to be a Genius too. It provides a safety net for every action in the game.
*   **Diligent**
    *   *Cost:* Moderate
    *   *Effect:* +1 to All Attributes. Boosts Stewardship.
    *   *Why:* The single best personality trait in the game. It unlocks special event options that almost always succeed.
*   **Patient**
    *   *Cost:* Moderate
    *   *Effect:* +Defense, +Diplomacy, +Stewardship, +Intrigue, +Learning.
    *   *Why:* In battle, this makes you a defensive specialist (great for surviving). In court, it boosts stats across the board.
*   **Just**
    *   *Cost:* Low
    *   *Effect:* +2 Stewardship, +Learning, +Vassal Opinion.
    *   *Why:* Vassals love a Just ruler. This prevents factions from firing early on.

### 4. Negative Traits (The Age Reducers)
To keep your character from starting at age 50, we need to take "bad" traits. These are chosen specifically because their downsides are manageable for a new player.

*   **Wounded**
    *   *Effect:* -1 Health, lowers stats slightly.
    *   *The Trick:* This dramatically lowers your starting age (making you younger). The "Wounded" trait usually heals within the first 3-6 months of gameplay, leaving you with a "Scarred" trait which gives you Prestige. It is essentially **free points**.
*   **Lustful**
    *   *Effect:* +25% Fertility, slight Piety loss.
    *   *Why:* In CK2, the game ends if you have no heir. Lustful ensures you pump out children. The Piety hit is negligible.
*   **Greedy**
    *   *Effect:* +10% Tax income, -Diplomacy.
    *   *Why:* We want money. The diplomacy hit is offset by the *Genius* trait.
*   **Legitimized Bastard** (Optional, if you need to lower age further)
    *   *Effect:* -1 Diplomacy.
    *   *Why:* If you are an independent ruler, nobody cares if you are a bastard. It’s free points.

### The Final Result

If you input this correctly, you should be starting between the ages of **18 and 23**.

**Estimated Final Stats (approximate):**
*   **Diplomacy:** ~10 (Decent. Vassals won't hate you.)
*   **Martial:** ~14 (Good. You can lead armies, but you aren't Alexander the Great.)
*   **Stewardship:** ~20+ (Excellent. You will be rich and can hold 6-8 counties personally.)
*   **Intrigue:** ~8 (Okay. Just keeps your Spymaster happy.)
*   **Learning:** ~8 (Low. You won't be building tech fast, but you don't need to yet.)

### Strategy for this Build

1.  **Day 1:** Pick the **Business Focus** (requires Way of Life DLC) or **Stewardship Focus**. This will trigger events to make you money.
2.  **The Wound:** Wait a few months for your "Wounded" trait to heal. Do not go to war or lead armies until the wound is gone, or you might die immediately.
3.  **Marriage:** Because you are a **Genius**, try to marry someone with the **Strong** or **Genius** trait to breed a super-heir. Because you are **Lustful** and a **Midas Touched**, you will have many children.
4.  **Expansion:** Use your high Stewardship to hold every county you conquer personally (up to your limit). Do not give land to vassals until you hit your limit (usually 6 or 7 holdings). This ensures you get 100% of the levies and tax from those lands.

### Where to play?
For this build, start in **Ireland** (The Tutorial Island) in the **1066 start date** (Petty King of Mumu is the classic choice). This build allows you to hold almost half of Ireland personally without needing vassals, making unifying the island very satisfying.
