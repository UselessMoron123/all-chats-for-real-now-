Choosing a good dynasty name in Crusader Kings II depends on your culture, your starting location, and the "vibe" you want for your playthrough.

Here is a breakdown of custom dynasty names categorized by style and region:

### 1. The "Noble & Ancient" Style (Generic Western)
These sound established and prestigious, perfect for a high-ranking Count or Duke.
*   **Vandemar:** Sounds Germanic/Frankish; implies strength.
*   **de Clarance:** A classic French-style house.
*   **Valerius:** For those claiming a fake (or real) Roman lineage.
*   **Aethelgard:** Perfect for an Anglo-Saxon lord.
*   **Ironwood:** For a sturdy, northern-style family.
*   **Beaufort:** "Beautiful Fortress" in French.

### 2. The "Ruthless & Dark" Style
Best for players who plan on murdering their way to the top of the Byzantine or Holy Roman Empire.
*   **Morgrave:** Sounds ominous and authoritative.
*   **Nacht:** German for "Night."
*   **Drax:** Short, punchy, and villainous.
*   **Vane:** Simple, elegant, and cold.
*   **Thorne:** Classic, sharp, and suggests a family that is hard to get rid of.

### 3. Culture-Specific Names
Matching the linguistic patterns of your region makes the game feel much more immersive.

*   **Norse (Viking):**
    *   **Siegfridsson:** Simple patronymic.
    *   **Storm-Eye:** Use a "Nickname" style as a dynasty name.
    *   **Wolf-Skin:** Great for a Berzerker lineage.
    *   **Hrolfsson:** Traditional and rugged.
*   **Byzantine (Greek):**
    *   **Strategos:** Literally "General."
    *   **Kallistos:** "The Most Beautiful."
    *   **Draconis:** A Latin-Greek hybrid for "Dragon."
    *   **Arkonis:** Sounds like a ruling class name.
*   **Celtic (Irish/Scottish/Welsh):**
    *   **O’Finnigan:** Classic Irish.
    *   **MacAlistair:** "Son of the Defender."
    *   **Pendragon:** If you are trying to reclaim Britannia as a Welshman.
    *   **Dunstan:** Old English/Celtic feel.
*   **Iberian (Spanish/Portuguese):**
    *   **de la Vega:** "Of the Meadow."
    *   **de Luna:** "Of the Moon."
    *   **Cortes:** Very formal and regal.

### 4. Based on Landmarks (The "Locative" Method)
Historically, many dynasties took names from the land they owned.
*   **[Town Name] + er:** e.g., if you own Hamburg, be **Hamburger**.
*   **von [Castle Name]:** e.g., **von Rheinfels**.
*   **de [River Name]:** e.g., **de Loire**.

### 5. Fantasy-Inspired (Subtle)
Names that sound like they could be from *A Song of Ice and Fire* or *The Witcher* without being too "meta."
*   **Hightower**
*   **Blackwood**
*   **Redwyne**
*   **Castellane**
*   **Tenebris**

### Pro-Tips for CK2 Dynasty Naming:
*   **The "De" Factor:** If you are playing French, Italian, or Spanish, remember that the game will often put your dynasty name after your title (e.g., King Louis *of* [Dynasty]). If you name your dynasty "de Valois," it might show up as "King Louis of de Valois," which looks messy. Check your culture's naming conventions first.
*   **Check the COA:** Match your name to your Coat of Arms. If your name is **Fox**, put a fox on your shield!
*   **Dynasty Motto:** If you have the *Customization Pack* DLC, you can’t set a motto, but you can imagine one to help pick the name. "House Ironwood: We Do Not Bend."
