In *Crusader Kings 2*, a good dynasty name usually depends on the culture you are playing and the "vibe" of your campaign.

Here is a breakdown of custom dynasty names categorized by region, style, and meaning:

### 1. Central & Western Europe (Frankish, German, English)
These often use the prefix **"de"** (French), **"von"** (German), or **"of"** (English) followed by a landmark or a fictional estate.

*   **De Rochefort** (Of the Strong Rock)
*   **Von Adlerstein** (Of Eagle Stone)
*   **Of Ashbourne** (Great for a Saxon/English start)
*   **De Martel** (The Hammer – great for a martial-focused lineage)
*   **Von Hochberg** (High Mountain)
*   **Beaufort** (Beautiful Fortress)
*   **Iron-Side** (Classic English/Saxon epithet)

### 2. Northern Europe (Norse/Scandinavian)
Norse names usually end in **-ing** (descendant of) or utilize powerful animal/nature imagery.

*   **Skallagrim** (Bald Grimace)
*   **Wulfung** (Sons of the Wolf)
*   **Ormr** (The Serpent)
*   **Storm-Eye**
*   **Hrolfsson** (If starting as a son of a Hrolf)
*   **Iron-Oak**
*   **Bear-Claw** (Bjorn-Klo)

### 3. Byzantine & Greek (Eastern Roman)
Byzantine names often sound prestigious and end in **-os** or **-as**.

*   **Argyros** (The Silvery)
*   **Chrysos** (The Golden)
*   **Kallergis** (Beautiful Worker)
*   **Draconas** (The Dragon)
*   **Asteris** (The Star)
*   **Strategos** (The General)

### 4. Mediterranean (Italian, Spanish, Occitan)
Use **"di"** (Italian) or **"de"** (Spanish).

*   **Di Valenti** (The Brave)
*   **De la Vega** (Of the Meadow)
*   **Di Fierro** (Of Iron)
*   **De Calatrava**
*   **Di Sangue** (Of Blood - a bit edgy, but fits a ruthless playthrough)

### 5. Middle Eastern & African (Arabic/Berber)
Most use the prefix **Banu** (Sons of) or **al-**.

*   **Banu Harb** (Sons of War)
*   **Al-Saif** (The Sword)
*   **Banu Qamar** (Sons of the Moon)
*   **Al-Zaki** (The Pure/Purebred)

### 6. Badass/Fantasy Style
If you aren't worried about strict historical accuracy and want something that looks cool on the map:

*   **Blackwood**
*   **Crown-Breaker**
*   **Vanguard**
*   **Morningstar**
*   **Dread-Mane**
*   **Silver-Tongue** (Great for a high-diplomacy/intrigue run)

### Pro-Tip: Using Prefixes in CK2
When you create your dynasty in the Customizer, remember that the game often adds "of" or "de" automatically depending on your culture. 

*   **Check the "Prefix" box:** If you want your dynasty to be "von Habsburg," put "Habsburg" in the name box and check the prefix box.
*   **Dynasty Shields:** If you pick a "High Medieval" name like **De Rochefort**, try to use a castle or a rock in your Coat of Arms for extra flavor.
