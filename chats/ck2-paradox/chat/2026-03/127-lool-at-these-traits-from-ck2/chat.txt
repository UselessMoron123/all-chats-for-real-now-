Here are the **Personality Traits** in Crusader Kings II, organized by the primary attribute they affect or the education path they are most associated with.

In CK2, most personality traits come in **Opposing Pairs**. You cannot hold both at the same time (e.g., if you are Brave and an event makes you Craven, you lose Brave).

### 👑 The "God Tier" Traits (Affect All Stats)
These are the two most critical personality traits in the game. They define whether a character is a powerhouse or a failure.

**Ambitious** (Opposite: Content)
*   **Stats:** All Attributes **+2**, Combat Rating **+5**
*   **Effect:** The best trait in the game for raw stats. However, having ambitious vassals is dangerous as they will want your titles.

**Content** (Opposite: Ambitious)
*   **Stats:** Intrigue **-1**
*   **Effect:** Monthly Piety +0.5. Vassals with this trait basically never revolt.

**Diligent** (Opposite: Slothful)
*   **Stats:** All Attributes **+1**
*   **Effect:** Hidden bonus to education outcomes. Essential for teaching children.

**Slothful** (Opposite: Diligent)
*   **Stats:** All Attributes **-1**
*   **Effect:** Lower levy reinforcement. A terrible trait.

---

### ⚔️ Martial Traits (Warfare & Combat)

**Brave** (Opposite: Craven)
*   **Stats:** Martial **+2**, Combat Rating **+10**
*   **Effect:** Vassal Opinion +5. Essential for commanders.

**Craven** (Opposite: Brave)
*   **Stats:** Martial **-2**, Combat Rating **-10**
*   **Effect:** Vassal Opinion -5. You will likely die in battle or flee, destroying your army's morale.

**Wroth** (Opposite: Patient)
*   **Stats:** Martial **+3**, Diplomacy **-1**, Intrigue **-1**
*   **Effect:** Good for commanders, bad for realm stability.

**Cruel** (Opposite: Kind)
*   **Stats:** Intrigue **+1**, Diplomacy **-1**
*   **Effect:** Morale Damage **+10%**, Combat Rating **+5**. Your vassals will hate you, but your enemies will fear you.

---

### 🕊️ Diplomacy Traits (Relations & Vassals)

**Gregarious** (Opposite: Shy)
*   **Stats:** Diplomacy **+2**
*   **Effect:** Sex Appeal +5, Vassal Opinion +5. One of the best traits for keeping a realm stable.

**Shy** (Opposite: Gregarious)
*   **Stats:** Diplomacy **-2**
*   **Effect:** Personal Combat Skill **-5**. A painful trait that makes everyone dislike you.

**Charitable** (Opposite: Greedy)
*   **Stats:** Diplomacy **+3**, Stewardship **-2**
*   **Effect:** Same Religion Opinion +5. Good for opinion, bad for income.

**Kind** (Opposite: Cruel/Envious)
*   **Stats:** Diplomacy **+2**, Intrigue **-2**
*   **Effect:** Vassal Opinion +5. Makes plots harder to pull off, but people love you.

**Humble** (Opposite: Proud)
*   **Stats:** Piety **+1/month**
*   **Effect:** Same Religion Opinion +5.

**Proud** (Opposite: Humble)
*   **Stats:** Prestige **+0.5/month**
*   **Effect:** No attribute bonus, just prestige.

---

### 💰 Stewardship Traits (Money & Management)

**Temperate** (Opposite: Gluttonous)
*   **Stats:** Stewardship **+2**
*   **Effect:** **Health +0.25**. This is a top-tier trait because the Stewardship boosts tax, and the health boost lets you reign longer.

**Gluttonous** (Opposite: Temperate)
*   **Stats:** Stewardship **-2**
*   **Effect:** Sex Appeal -10, Health -0.25. (Historically, the "Fat" trait).

**Greedy** (Opposite: Charitable)
*   **Stats:** Stewardship **+2**, Diplomacy **-1**
*   **Effect:** National Tax Modifier **+10%**. The best trait for making money, but vassals will dislike you (-5).

**Just** (Opposite: Arbitrary)
*   **Stats:** Stewardship **+2**, Learning **+1**
*   **Effect:** Vassal Opinion +5. Excellent all-rounder trait.

**Arbitrary** (Opposite: Just)
*   **Stats:** Stewardship **-2**, Learning **-1**
*   **Effect:** Vassal Opinion -5.

**Stubborn** (No direct opposite, sometimes acts against Content)
*   **Stats:** Stewardship **+1**, Diplomacy **-1**

---

### 🕵️ Intrigue Traits (Plots & Spies)

**Deceitful** (Opposite: Honest)
*   **Stats:** Intrigue **+3**, Diplomacy **-2**
*   **Effect:** Essential for Spymasters and assassinations.

**Honest** (Opposite: Deceitful)
*   **Stats:** Diplomacy **+3**, Intrigue **-2**
*   **Effect:** Terrible for plotting, but great for state diplomacy.

**Envious** (Opposite: Kind)
*   **Stats:** Intrigue **+2**, Diplomacy **-1**
*   **Effect:** Liege Opinion -15. If your vassal is Envious, they *will* try to kill you or ruin your day.

**Paranoid** (Opposite: Trusting)
*   **Stats:** Intrigue **+2**, Diplomacy **-1**
*   **Effect:** Helps you discover plots against yourself, but makes it hard to pass diplomacy checks.

**Trusting** (Opposite: Paranoid)
*   **Stats:** Diplomacy **+1**, Intrigue **-2**
*   **Effect:** Makes it very easy for people to assassinate you.

### 📚 Learning Traits (Religion & Tech)

**Zealous** (Opposite: Cynical)
*   **Stats:** Martial **+2**
*   **Effect:** Piety +1.0/month. Same Religion Opinion +5, Different Religion Opinion -50. You cannot study technology or be friendly with infidels.

**Cynical** (Opposite: Zealous)
*   **Stats:** Intrigue **+2**
*   **Effect:** Piety -0.2/month. Allows you to ignore religious restrictions and is better for tech growth.

**Patient** (Opposite: Wroth)
*   **Stats:** Diplomacy **+1**, Stewardship **+1**, Learning **+1**, Defense **+20%**
*   **Effect:** One of the most underrated traits. The defense bonus helps in battle, and the spread of stats is excellent.

**Chaste** (Opposite: Lustful)
*   **Stats:** Learning **+1**
*   **Effect:** Fertility **-15%**. Piety +0.5.

**Lustful** (Opposite: Chaste)
*   **Stats:** Intrigue **+1**
*   **Effect:** Fertility **+25%**. Piety -0.25. Essential if you need an heir quickly or are playing a seduction game.
