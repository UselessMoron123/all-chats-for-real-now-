For a **Balanced Ruler** who starts with a Martial education, the choice of Lifestyle Trait determines your playstyle flavor.

Since you **must have Ambitious** (which is excellent, giving +2 to all stats), here are your best Lifestyle considerations based on your definitions:

### Top 3 Lifestyle Options

**1. The "Siege Master" (Recommended for Balance)**
*   **Trait:** `architect`
*   **Stats:** Stewardship +2, Martial +1.
*   **Why:** It bridges the gap perfectly. You get high Stewardship for your demesne, but the **+20% Siege Speed** is incredible for a Martial character. You will win wars significantly faster, which saves money and manpower.

**2. The "Empire Builder" (Pure Stats)**
*   **Trait:** `administrator`
*   **Stats:** Stewardship +3.
*   **Why:** If you want the maximum possible Demesne limit and tax income, this is the math winner. The movement speed helps your armies chase down rebels.

**3. The "Battlefield Genius" (Tactical)**
*   **Trait:** `game_master`
*   **Stats:** Diplomacy +2, Martial +1.
*   **Why:** If you are worried about your low Diplomacy (since Martial education gives 0 Diplo), this helps vassals like you. More importantly, the **Narrow Flank** modifier is a "God Tactic" that lets you win battles even when outnumbered.

### The Code (Architect Build)
I have selected **Architect** here because it fits a "Balanced Warrior" best—you build your realm during peace (Stewardship) and crush forts quickly during war (Siege).

I also ensured **Ambitious** is included.

```pdx
2666000 = {
	name = "MyRuler"
	religion = baltic_pagan
	culture = prussian
	easter_egg = yes
	diplomacy = 1
	martial = 1
	stewardship = 1
	intrigue = 1
	learning = 1
	
	# --- EDUCATION (Tier 1 -> Will grow to Tier 4) ---
	add_trait = misguided_warrior
	
	# --- FOUNDATION ---
	add_trait = genius
	add_trait = shrewd
	add_trait = strong
	add_trait = attractive
	
	# --- LIFESTYLE (The "Balanced" Choice) ---
	add_trait = architect
	# Gives +20% Siege Speed (War) AND +2 Stewardship (Peace)
	
	# --- PERSONALITY (Limit: 5) ---
	add_trait = ambitious   # REQUIRED: +2 All, +3 Combat
	add_trait = diligent    # +1 All
	add_trait = patient     # +1 All (except Martial), +20% Defence
	add_trait = just        # +2 Stw, +5 Vassal Opinion
	add_trait = temperate   # +2 Stw, +5 Church Opinion

	disallow_random_traits = yes
	753.1.1 = {
		birth = yes
		employer = 194074
	}
	820.1.1 = {
		death = yes
	}
}
```
