Based on the stats provided, the "best" combination for a universal ruler focuses on **Stewardship** (to hold more land and make more money), **Martial** (to win wars), and **Vassal Management** (to prevent revolts).

Here are the 5 best traits to create the ultimate ruler:

### 1. Ambitious
*   **Stats:** Martial +2, Intrigue +2, Diplomacy +2, Stewardship +2, Learning +2, Combat Rating +3
*   **Why:** This is the single best trait in the list. It provides a massive +2 bonus to **every single attribute**. In a game of percentages and thresholds, a flat +10 total stat boost is unmatched.

### 2. Diligent
*   **Stats:** Martial +1, Intrigue +1, Diplomacy +1, Stewardship +1, Learning +1, Vassal Opinion +5
*   **Why:** Like Ambitious, this gives a "flat" boost to every attribute. More importantly, the **+5 Vassal Opinion** is a universal stabilizer, making every person in your realm slightly less likely to plot against you.

### 3. Brilliant Strategist (Education)
*   **Stats:** Martial +9, Intrigue +2, Stewardship +2, Learning -1, Health +0.5, Combat Rating +20
*   **Why:** You need one high-tier education. While *Grey Eminence* is great for diplomacy, *Brilliant Strategist* is superior because of the **+0.5 Health**. Longevity is the most important "hidden" stat; staying alive for an extra 5–10 years allows you to finish long-term projects and avoid the instability of frequent successions. The +9 Martial also ensures you have a massive personal levy.

### 4. Patient
*   **Stats:** Intrigue +1, Diplomacy +1, Stewardship +1, Learning +1, Combat Rating +5
*   **Why:** This is a "buffer" trait. It provides a boost to four out of five major stats and makes your character better in a duel. It has no negative trade-offs, making it strictly better than its counterparts like *Wroth* or *Kind*.

### 5. Just
*   **Stats:** Stewardship +2, Learning +1
*   **Why:** Stewardship is the most "universal" stat for a ruler because it determines your **Domain Limit** (how many castles/cities you can own personally). Owning more land yourself means you are richer and more powerful than your vassals, which is the best way to prevent civil wars. 

---

### Total Stat Bonuses from this Combination:
*   **Martial:** +12 (Huge armies and battle performance)
*   **Stewardship:** +7 (Can hold 1–2 extra pieces of land personally)
*   **Diplomacy:** +4 (Decent relations)
*   **Intrigue:** +6 (Good protection against assassins)
*   **Learning:** +4 (Decent technology growth)
*   **Combat Rating:** +28 (Almost invincible in personal combat)
*   **Passive:** +0.5 Health and +5 Vassal Opinion.

**Summary:** This ruler would be a powerhouse who lives a long time, holds a large amount of personal land, and is nearly impossible to defeat on the battlefield or via assassination.
