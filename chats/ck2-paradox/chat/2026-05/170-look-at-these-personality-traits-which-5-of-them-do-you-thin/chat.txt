cool prompt. using only the modifiers you posted, here’s the most “universal” 5-trait package that plays well with any education and plugs the typical gaps:

- Ambitious — +2 to every stat, +3 combat rating. Instantly raises your floor and cancels any -1 from education.
- Diligent — +1 to every stat, +5 vassal opinion. Stability and across-the-board competence.
- Patient — +1 Intrigue/Diplomacy/Stewardship/Learning, +5 combat rating. Rounds out the non‑martial stats and adds survivability.
- Just — +2 Stewardship, +1 Learning. Great economy/tech padding; specifically fixes the common -1 Stewardship/Learning holes.
- Brave — +2 Martial, +10 combat rating. Covers weak martial educations and keeps you alive if you ever lead troops or duel.

What this gives you baseline (before education)
- Martial: +5
- Intrigue: +4
- Diplomacy: +4
- Stewardship: +6
- Learning: +5
- Combat Rating: +18
- Vassal Opinion: +5

Why this works across all educations
- Elusive Shadow (Intrigue 9, -1 Stewardship): Just + Diligent + Patient wipe the -1 Stewardship and add big DIP/LEA. Brave prevents you from being a glass cannon.
- Grey Eminence (Diplomacy 9, -1 Martial): Brave (+2) + Ambitious (+2) + Diligent (+1) fix Martial immediately; you also gain meaningful INT/STEW/LEA so you’re not only a face.
- Midas Touched (Stewardship 9, -1 Diplomacy): Ambitious + Diligent + Patient give you +4 DIP to erase the -1. Brave gives you teeth for wars you’ll fund.
- Brilliant Strategist (Martial 9, -1 Learning): Just (+1 LEA) + Ambitious (+2) + Diligent (+1) + Patient (+1) overfix Learning while adding INT/DIP/STEW depth; Brave’s CR is gravy for battlefield safety.
- Mastermind Theologian (Learning 9, -1 Intrigue): Ambitious + Diligent + Patient = +4 Intrigue to erase the -1, plus Martial/CR from Brave so you’re not helpless militarily.
- Misguided Warrior (-1 Learning): Same story—Just/Diligent/Patient/Ambitious cover Learning and overall competence.

If you prefer a peacetime/econ tilt
- Swap Brave -> Greedy for +10% global tax (-1 DIP is easily covered by the others). This is huge if you rarely lead armies yourself.
- Or swap Brave -> Gregarious if you want extra smooth diplomacy without any combat focus.

Traits I wouldn’t include for a “universal” kit
- Zealous (big cross-faith opinion malus), Deceitful/Paranoid/Cruel (hurt Diplomacy), Kind/Honest/Charitable (heavy combat/intrigue penalties), Wroth (DIP/INT hit). Great situationally, but not plug‑and‑play.

Net: Ambitious + Diligent + Patient are your core “always-on” trio; Just is the best all-round econ/tech patch; Brave (or Greedy if you’re a pure builder) rounds it out.
