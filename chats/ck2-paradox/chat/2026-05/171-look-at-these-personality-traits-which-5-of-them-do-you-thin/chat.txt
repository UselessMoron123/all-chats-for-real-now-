To create a "universal" ruler that compensates for the specific weaknesses of any education (the -1 stat penalties) while maximizing the strengths of the main attribute, you need traits that offer across-the-board stat boosts and stability.

Here are the 5 best traits to form a "Universal Powerhouse" combination:

### 1. Ambitious
*   **Stats:** +2 Martial, +2 Intrigue, +2 Diplomacy, +2 Stewardship, +2 Learning, +3 Combat Rating.
*   **Why:** This is the single most important trait. It provides a massive +2 to every single category. This immediately turns every education's "dump stat" (the -1 penalty) into a +1 advantage. It is the foundation of a "jack-of-all-trades" ruler.

### 2. Diligent
*   **Stats:** +1 Martial, +1 Intrigue, +1 Diplomacy, +1 Stewardship, +1 Learning, +5 Vassal Opinion.
*   **Why:** Combined with Ambitious, you now have +3 in every stat. Furthermore, the **+5 Vassal Opinion** is the best "universal" modifier for any ruler. Whether you are a tyrant (Intrigue) or a Saint (Learning), having vassals who hate you less makes the game significantly easier.

### 3. Patient
*   **Stats:** +1 Martial, +1 Intrigue, +1 Diplomacy, +1 Stewardship, +1 Learning, +5 Combat Rating.
*   **Why:** This completes the "Trinity" of stat-boosting traits. With Ambitious, Diligent, and Patient, your ruler has a base of **+4 to every single stat** before education is even factored in. The +5 Combat Rating also helps non-martial rulers survive battle or duels.

### 4. Just
*   **Stats:** +2 Stewardship, +1 Learning.
*   **Why:** Stewardship is arguably the most important "secondary" stat for any ruler because it increases your domain limit (how many castles you can hold) and your tax income. Since most educations (except Midas Touched) don't focus on Stewardship, *Just* ensures you have the money and land to fund your wars or bribes.

### 5. Brave
*   **Stats:** +2 Martial, +10 Combat Rating.
*   **Why:** Personal survival and military lead are vital. Even if you are a "Grey Eminence" (Diplomat) or "Mastermind Theologian" (Scholar), you will eventually be dragged into a war. Brave gives a massive boost to your survivability and your ability to win battles, compensating for the low Martial scores of the non-combat educations.
