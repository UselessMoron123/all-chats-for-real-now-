Based on the stats provided (which mirror the mechanics of *Crusader Kings II*), the best universal combination for a ruler focuses on maximizing **Stewardship** (for money and land), **Martial** (for army size), and **Vassal Opinion** (for stability).

Here are the 5 best traits/educations for a "Universal Ruler":

### 1. Ambitious (The "Must-Have")
*   **Stats:** +2 Martial, +2 Intrigue, +2 Diplomacy, +2 Stewardship, +2 Learning, +3 Combat Rating.
*   **Why:** This is the single most powerful trait in the list. It provides a massive +2 bonus to every single attribute. In a game of numbers, there is no other trait that offers this much raw power with zero downside for a primary ruler.

### 2. Brilliant Strategist (The Education)
*   **Stats:** +9 Martial, +2 Intrigue, +2 Stewardship, -1 Learning, +0.5 Health, +20 Combat Rating.
*   **Why:** While *Midas Touched* and *Grey Eminence* are great, *Brilliant Strategist* is the best for survival. The +9 Martial significantly increases your personal levy size (more troops), and the +20 Combat Rating paired with +0.5 Health means your ruler is much harder to kill in battle or through natural causes.

### 3. Diligent
*   **Stats:** +1 Martial, +1 Intrigue, +1 Diplomacy, +1 Stewardship, +1 Learning, **+5 Vassal Opinion**.
*   **Why:** Like a "mini-ambitious," it boosts every stat by +1. However, the real value is the **+5 Vassal Opinion**. Happy vassals provide more taxes and more troops while being less likely to join factions against you.

### 4. Patient
*   **Stats:** +1 Intrigue, +1 Diplomacy, +1 Stewardship, +1 Learning, +5 Combat Rating.
*   **Why:** This trait has no negatives. It provides a clean +1 to four out of five major stats and a nice boost to your personal combat ability. It balances out the ruler by making them more competent in administrative and diplomatic tasks.

### 5. Just
*   **Stats:** +2 Stewardship, +1 Learning.
*   **Why:** Stewardship is arguably the most important stat for a ruler because it determines how many personal holdings (castles/cities) you can own directly. More land equals more personal wealth and power that isn't dependent on vassals. *Just* provides a solid boost here with no stat penalties.

---

### Summary of the Total Bonuses:
If you have all 5 of these, your ruler gains:
*   **Martial:** +12 (Massive army size and damage)
*   **Stewardship:** +8 (Huge income and higher demesne limit)
*   **Intrigue:** +6 (Better at discovering plots against you)
*   **Diplomacy:** +4 (Better relations)
*   **Learning:** +4 (Faster technology spread)
*   **Combat Rating:** +28 (Nearly invincible in personal combat)
*   **Other:** +5 Vassal Opinion and +0.5 Health.

**Honorable Mention:** If you find your kingdom is constantly revolting, you could swap **Just** for **Gregarious** (+2 Diplomacy, Sex Appeal) to further boost how much people like you.
