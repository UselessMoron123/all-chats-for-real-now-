# Best Universal 5-Trait Combination for Any Ruler

## My Selection: **Diligent, Ambitious, Patient, Honest, Just**

### Why This Works:

**Diligent** (+1 to all skills, +5 vassal opinion)
- The foundation: boosts everything without drawbacks
- Exceptional vassal relations are universally valuable

**Ambitious** (+2 to all skills, +3 combat rating)
- Reinforces diligent's all-around boost
- Adds +3 combat for military credibility
- No negative effects

**Patient** (+1 to 4 skills, +5 combat rating, no diplomacy penalty)
- Compensates for combat rating weaknesses in other traits
- Adds stewardship/learning/diplomacy/intrigue all equally
- Prevents reckless decisions

**Honest** (+3 diplomacy, -2 intrigue)
- Fixes the diplomacy gap from education traits
- Builds trust with vassals and allies
- The -2 intrigue is offset by having diligent + ambitious

**Just** (+2 stewardship, +1 learning)
- Boosts stewardship (often the weakest education attribute)
- Supports realm stability
- Pairs naturally with honest for legitimacy

### Why NOT the "edgy" options:
- **Wroth/Cruel/Deceitful**: Destroy diplomacy, create vassal/ally problems
- **Greedy**: Tax penalty contradicts stewardship focus
- **Kind/Charitable**: Combat rating penalties too severe

This combination is **stable, well-rounded, and lacks crippling drawbacks**.
