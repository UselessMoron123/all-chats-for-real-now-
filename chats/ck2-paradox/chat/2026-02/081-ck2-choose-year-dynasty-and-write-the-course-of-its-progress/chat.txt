Here is a gameplay-focused progression log (AAR) for one of the most classic "Return to Glory" challenges in CK2.

**Start Date:** 1066 (High Middle Ages)
**Character:** Count Herbert Karling of Vermandois
**Dynasty:** Karling (The last landed male descendant of Charlemagne)
**Goal:** Reclaim the Empire of Francia and the HRE.

***

### Generation 1: Count Herbert "The Rat" Karling (1066–1090)
**Situation:** I am a single-county Count under the King of France. Everyone hates me because I have a claim on the Kingdom, but no army to back it up. The Capet Kings want my land.

*   **1066:** Immediately pick the **Seduction Focus**. I need a lot of kids for alliances, and my wife is mediocre. I set my Chancellor to fabricate a claim on the neighboring County of Amiens.
*   **1067:** Borrow 300 gold from Jewish merchants. Expel the Jews for another 200 gold. I need merc money.
*   **1068:** Fabrication succeeds (20% RNG, lucky). I declare war on the Count of Amiens. I hire the cheapest mercenary band. I stack wipe him. I am now a double-count.
*   **1070:** King Philippe of France demands I switch to Gavelkind. I refuse. He doesn't revoke because he’s busy losing a war to William the Conqueror.
*   **1075:** My Chancellor is now parked in Paris to "Improve Relations" so the King doesn't crush me. Meanwhile, I am plotting to kill the Duke of Valois.
*   **1078:** Plot power at 140%. The Duke dies of "suspicious manure explosion." His child takes over. I immediately fabricate a claim on the Duchy title.
*   **1085:** I usurp the Duchy of Valois. I am now a Duke. I betroth my heir, Eudes, to the third daughter of the Holy Roman Emperor (HRE) to secure a Non-Aggression Pact.
*   **1090:** Herbert dies of stress.

### Generation 2: Duke Eudes "The Breeder" Karling (1090–1125)
**Situation:** Moderate power Duke. Protected by HRE alliance. Time to play the genetics game.

*   **1090:** I switch to **Intrigue Focus**. My wife (the Kaiser's daughter) has a weak claim on the HRE. I need to make that claim useful for our son.
*   **1095:** I start a murder spree in the HRE. I kill the Kaiser’s two sons. Plot power is low, so I burn a lot of gold on bribes.
*   **1100:** My wife’s father dies. Her younger brother takes the throne. My wife now has a strong claim? No, still weak. Whatever. I focus on internal French politics.
*   **1105:** I start a faction in France: "Elective Monarchy." The AI King hates this. I force the revolt when the King is crusading in Jerusalem.
*   **1108:** We win. France is now Elective. I am the most powerful Duke, but the electors prefer the Duke of Aquitaine.
*   **1115:** I focus on Economy. I build Castle Towns in every holding. I need raw income for retinue cap. I invest in Heavy Infantry (Generic Shock Retinue).
*   **1120:** I notice the genius trait in a lowborn courtier. I take her as a lover. Legitimized a genius bastard son, Pepin. He is the new heir. Sorry, legitimate firstborn son, you have the "Slow" trait. I order you to take vows and become a monk.
*   **1125:** Eudes dies of "Natural Causes" (Age 55).

### Generation 3: Duke (later King) Pepin "The Usurper" Karling (1125–1155)
**Situation:** Genius stats (18 Martial, 20 Stewardship). It's go time.

*   **1126:** The King of France is excommunicated by the Pope because of a Free Investiture dispute. This is my window.
*   **1127:** I request a sanctioned invasion from the Pope. He agrees because I have high piety and the King is excommunicated.
*   **1128:** I declare war for the Kingdom of France. I call in my allies (Duke of Flanders and a random Duke from Burgundy).
*   **1130:** The war is a grind. The King has more troops, but the AI is stupid and takes attrition chasing a small stack of my troops in the mountains. I siege Paris.
*   **1131:** 100% Warscore. **I am now King of France.**
*   **1135:** Cleanup phase. I revoke titles from every vassal who fought against me. I invite high-martial characters to court to lead my armies.
*   **1140:** I look at the HRE. It's a mess. Border gore everywhere.
*   **1145:** I invite a claimant to the Kingdom of Aquitaine to my court. I land him with a barony. I press his claim.
*   **1150:** Aquitaine is added to the realm. I now control almost all of modern France.
*   **1155:** Pepin dies in battle (bad RNG on a duel event).

### Generation 4: Emperor Charles "The Spider" Karling (1155–1190)
**Situation:** Double King (France/Aquitaine). Goal: Empire.

*   **1155:** Threat level is high (50%). Defensive pacts are forming. I can't war openly for a while.
*   **1160:** I appoint an Anti-Pope. The real Pope excommunicates me. I don't care.
*   **1162:** I declare war on the Papacy to press my Anti-Pope's claim.
*   **1164:** I capture Rome. My Anti-Pope is now the real Pope and, crucially, he is my vassal.
*   **Gameplay Loop Exploit:** I can now ask the Pope for claims on *any* Catholic Duchy, and he will say yes because he is my vassal.
*   **1165–1175:** I claim the Duchy of Brabant, the Duchy of Savoy, and the Duchy of Lombardy. I press claims one by one. The defensive pacts trigger, but my Retinue stack (10k Heavy Infantry) plus Mercs is too strong.
*   **1180:** I have enough land. I click the button: **Create Empire of Francia.**
*   **1182:** The HRE is weak. I get a claim on the HRE title via the Pope (Sanctioned Invasion again).
*   **1185:** The Great Karling War. Francia vs HRE. It’s a massive lag-fest. 40k troops vs 40k troops.
*   **1188:** I catch the Kaiser in battle. He is captured. Warscore instantly 100%.
*   **1190:** I hold two Empires. I destroy the HRE title to remove the "Opinion of Predecessor" penalty and force de-jure drift into Francia.

### End State (1200)
**Map Mode:** A massive blue blob covering France, Germany, Northern Italy, and the Low Countries.
**Internal Politics:** Absolute Crown Authority. Vice-royalties handed out to content, old dynasty members who have no children.
**Score:** Karling Restoration complete. Game becomes boring map-painting from here on out. Time to quit and start a new run as a Zoroastrian count.
