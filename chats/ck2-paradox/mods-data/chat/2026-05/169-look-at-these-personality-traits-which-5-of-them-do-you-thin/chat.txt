If you want a **universal “works with any education” ruler build**, you’re basically trying to smooth out weaknesses while stacking broadly useful stats (especially diplomacy, stewardship, intrigue, and general opinion).

The strongest 5-trait core from your list is:

**ambitious + diligent + patient + temperate + just**

Why this combo works across all educations:

- **ambitious**: massive flat +2 to everything + combat. This is the single best “patch everything” trait.
- **diligent**: another all-stat boost + vassal opinion. It stacks perfectly with ambitious and has zero real downside.
- **patient**: fills intrigue/diplomacy/stewardship/learning and adds combat rating. No real penalties—insanely efficient.
- **temperate**: clean +2 stewardship, which is universally valuable (demesne size, income).
- **just**: +stewardship +learning, both always useful, especially to stabilize weaker educations.

What this does in practice:
- Covers **all five attributes** reliably
- Heavily boosts **stewardship** (important for every ruler type)
- Avoids **diplomacy penalties** (critical for vassal management)
- Avoids **intrigue penalties** (so you’re not vulnerable)
- Keeps **combat rating positive**, but not at the cost of governance

Why not include others:
- **charitable / kind / honest**: diplomacy is nice, but their combat/intrigue penalties hurt versatility
- **brave / wroth**: too combat-focused, not “universal ruler” material
- **deceitful / paranoid / cynical**: intrigue-heavy but come with diplomacy or piety downsides that hurt stability
- **gregarious**: decent, but weaker than the core 5 above in raw stat coverage

How it complements each education:
- **Elusive Shadow (intrigue-heavy)**: this fixes stewardship and diplomacy gaps
- **Grey Eminence (diplomacy-heavy)**: adds stewardship and general stat depth
- **Midas Touched (stewardship-heavy)**: rounds out diplomacy/intrigue
- **Brilliant Strategist (martial-heavy)**: compensates weak diplomacy/learning
- **Mastermind Theologian (learning-heavy)**: fixes intrigue deficit and boosts governance

If you wanted a slight variant depending on playstyle:
- Swap **just → gregarious** if you value diplomacy/opinion more than learning

But the first 5 is the most consistently optimal “plug-and-play ruler” setup.
