### 2. Diligent
*   **Stats:** +1 Martial, +1 Intrigue, +1 Diplomacy, +1 Stewardship, +1 Learning, +5 Vassal Opinion.
*   **Why it’s essential:** Similar to Ambitious, it provides a "flat" bonus to every single stat. However, the +5 Vassal Opinion is the real winner here. A ruler’s greatest threat is usually their own people; Diligent makes you more competent while simultaneously making your subordinates like you more.

### 3. Patient
*   **Stats:** +1 Intrigue, +1 Diplomacy, +1 Stewardship, +1 Learning, +5 Combat Rating.
*   **Why it’s essential:** While it lacks a Martial bonus, it provides a +5 Combat Rating (vital for not dying in battle) and boosts almost every other stat. In gameplay terms, "Patient" characters often get better event outcomes, and these stat boosts complement Diligent and Ambitious perfectly.

### 4. Just
*   **Stats:** +2 Stewardship, +1 Learning.
*   **Why it’s essential:** Stewardship is arguably the most important "ruler" stat because it determines your domain limit (how much land you can own personally) and your tax income. Between Ambitious, Diligent, Patient, and Just, you are gaining a massive **+6 bonus to Stewardship**, ensuring a wealthy and large personal demesne.

### 5. Brave
*   **Stats:** +2 Martial, +10 Combat Rating.
*   **Why it’s essential:** A ruler who dies in their first battle is a failed ruler. The +10 Combat Rating is the highest defensive/offensive boost on this list, significantly increasing your survivability. The +2 Martial also increases the size of your levies (your army), which deters vassals from rebelling.
