If we’re looking for the **most universal ruler** (works for war, peace, economy, intrigue, vassal control, religion), we want:

- No major diplomacy penalties (vassal stability is king)
- No major intrigue penalties (plots kill rulers)
- Strong stewardship (money = power)
- Good martial (deterrence + levies)
- Some learning (tech + religion)
- Minimal severe downsides (like −2 intrigue or −2 diplomacy)

---

# ✅ The 5 Best Universal Traits

### 1) **Ambitious** (mandatory pick)
> +2 to *everything*, +3 combat

This is by far the strongest all-round trait. No penalties. Pure ruler fuel.

✔ Works with every education  
✔ Strong in every playstyle  
✔ Boosts all core stats  

---

### 2) **Diligent**
> +1 to all stats, +5 vassal opinion

This is insanely good for stability.

✔ Helps literally everything  
✔ Vassal opinion is huge  
✔ No downside  

Ambitious + Diligent already makes a monster ruler.

---

### 3) **Patient**
> +1 intrigue, diplomacy, stewardship, learning  
> +5 combat rating  

This is extremely efficient:

✔ No penalties  
✔ Makes you harder to kill  
✔ Boosts economy + diplomacy + plots  

Patient is one of the most universally powerful traits in the list.

---

### 4) **Just**
> +2 stewardship, +1 learning  

Simple but powerful.

✔ Direct economic boost  
✔ No penalties  
✔ Pairs beautifully with Midas, Grey Eminence, Strategist  

Money and stability win long-term games.

---

### 5) **Temperate**
> +2 stewardship  

Boring but extremely effective.

✔ More demesne income  
✔ Larger domain limit synergy  
✔ No downside  

Stewardship is the most universally useful stat in CK-style systems.
