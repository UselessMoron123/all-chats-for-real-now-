Yes—CK2 bloodlines turn a dynastic RPG into a collectible card game.

Players obsess over them because they’re:

- **Permanent-ish power**: bonuses that persist across generations, often stacking (combat, prestige, fertility, vassal opinion, etc.).
- **Status trophies**: “I’m descended from Alexander / a saint / a legendary warrior” is its own win condition.
- **Optimization bait**: once you realize you can **breed and inherit multiple bloodlines**, it becomes a long-term planning puzzle (marriage webs, inheritance control, legitimizing bastards, etc.).
- **Narrative fuel**: they’re an easy way to justify absurd dynastic sagas—“the Blood of X runs in our veins” is instant headcanon.

Here are a few custom “bloodline collector” concepts that feel very CK2, with mechanics that reinforce the playstyle (marriage-webs, inheritance control, dynastic preservation) without just being raw stat creep. Mix-and-match as you like.

---

## 1) **Blood of the Genealogist-King** (learning/roleplay, “I catalog blood”)
**Flavor:** The founder compiled the *Great Register of Lineages*, proving descent, exposing frauds, and arranging “proper” unions to preserve ancient lines.

**Creation requirement (examples):**
- Has **3+ bloodlines** personally (or dynasty has 5+)
- **Learning 12+**, Scholar/Lunatic optional
- Completed a decision: **“Commission the Great Genealogy”** (cost gold + prestige; requires High/Absolute Crown Authority or just being independent)

**Bonuses (bloodline modifiers):**
- **+1 Learning**
- **+0.10 monthly prestige**
- **+10 opinion with dynasty members**
- **+5 opinion with characters who have a bloodline** (they respect the “recognized pedigree”)
- **+10 marriage acceptance** *when marrying into a bloodlined character/dynasty* (models your reputation as a careful matchmaker)

**Nice CK2-style special:**
- **Unique decision:** *“Publish a Genealogical Claim”*  
  Target a character whose parentage is “conveniently unclear” → you get a weak hook-style leverage (in CK2 terms: opinion modifier + possible claim fabrication event chain / legitimization pressure).

---

## 2) **Blood of the Dynastic Curator** (stewardship/long game, “I preserve lines from extinction”)
**Flavor:** Your house is known for taking in cadet branches, legitimizing heirs, and preventing great names from dying out.

**Creation requirement (examples):**
- Dynasty has **X living members** (e.g., 20+), and you’ve landed **3+ dynasty members**
- At least **one “rescued” marriage**: you married a lowborn/bastard/last-of-line bloodlined character into your dynasty

**Bonuses:**
- **+1 Stewardship**
- **+0.05 monthly prestige**
- **-10% build cost** (your “curation” comes with orderly administration)
- **+15 dynasty opinion**
- **+5 vassal opinion** *if vassal is dynasty* (encourages dynastic realm-building)

**Special event hook:**
- Periodic event: *“A Dying Line Seeks Shelter”*  
  A courtier/guest with a bloodline appears (or a generated “last scion” of some notable house). Options: arrange marriage (prestige), grant title (piety/prestige), or turn them away.

---

## 3) **Blood of the Blood-Broker** (diplomacy/intrigue, “I acquire bloodlines like artifacts”)
**Flavor:** Other nobles whisper that your dynasty doesn’t “inherit” greatness—your house *buys, bargains, and steals it* through marriage contracts, hostage-wards, and pressure.

**Creation requirement (examples):**
- Have **4+ bloodlines** and **20k+ prestige** (or high diplomacy + intrigue)
- Have successfully arranged **10+ marriages** (including for courtiers) and used favors/plots to enforce at least one

**Bonuses:**
- **+1 Diplomacy** (or +1 Intrigue, depending on theme)
- **+15% plot power** (optional, if you want it darker)
- **+15 marriage acceptance** (broadly)
- **+10 opinion with rulers** (they respect/need your matchmaking network)
- **-10 opinion with Zealous characters** (they distrust your “blood-trading”)

**Special decision:**
- *“Negotiate a Blood Contract”* (once per ruler lifetime): spend gold/prestige → get a strong marriage acceptance boost with a specific target realm for a short window.

---

## 4) **Blood of the Lineage-Purist** (for the “keep them, don’t dilute them” crowd)
**Flavor:** Your house treats bloodlines as sacred relics—protected, recorded, and never allowed to slip away.

**Creation requirement (examples):**
- Personally possess **3+ bloodlines**
- No illegitimate children **recognized** (or the opposite: you legitimized specifically to preserve bloodlines—pick your narrative)
- High piety OR high learning (depending on whether it’s religious or scholarly)

**Bonuses (careful—this can get spicy):**
- **+0.5 health** (survivability = preservation)
- **+10 same-dynasty opinion**
- **+5 same-religion opinion**
- Small **fertility bonus** (+5%) *or* small **attraction opinion** (to facilitate “needed” marriages)

**Optional drawback to keep it balanced:**
- **-5 general opinion** (others find you arrogant)
- **+10% stress-equivalent behavior** doesn’t exist in CK2, but you can simulate via events (more rivalries, more “scandal” events)

---

# A fun “collector meta” mechanic (if you’re willing to script events)
If you want the bloodline to *directly* care about collecting, add a hidden counter:

- Every time a dynasty member **gains a new bloodline**, fire an event:
  - Dynasty prestige boost
  - “The Archive Grows” modifier for 10 years: +prestige/month, +marriage acceptance
- At thresholds (e.g., 5 / 8 / 12 total bloodlines in dynasty), unlock tiered perks.

This makes the bloodline feel like it’s “leveling up” as you collect.
