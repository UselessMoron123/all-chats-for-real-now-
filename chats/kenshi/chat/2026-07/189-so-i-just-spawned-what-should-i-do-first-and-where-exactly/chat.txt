Welcome to **Oxygen Not Included**! You’ve spawned in a cold biome (likely the Rime asteroid or a Frosty DLC start), which adds a unique challenge: keeping things warm enough to grow food, while most of your water is currently frozen.

Here is exactly what you should do in the first 2-3 cycles:

### 1. Dig Out Your Living Space
First, use the **Dig (G)** tool. You want to create horizontal "floors" that are 4 tiles high. 
*   **Where:** Dig out to the left and right of your Printing Pod. This pod provides a little bit of light and a tiny bit of oxygen to start.
*   **Goal:** Create flat areas for your first rooms.

### 2. Sanitation (The Most Urgent Task)
If you don't build toilets, your Duplicants will make a mess on the floor, which leads to germs and stress.
*   **Where:** Dig a room a bit to the side (maybe to the right of the pod).
*   **What to build:** Go to the **Plumbing** tab and build **2 Outhouses** and **1 Wash Basin**. 
*   **Pro-Tip:** Make sure the Wash Basin is set to "Direction: Left" (or whichever way they exit) so they are forced to wash their hands after using the toilet.

### 3. Oxygen
You have some oxygen in the air now, but it will run out fast.
*   **What to build:** In the **Oxygen** tab, build an **Algae Deoxidizer** near your pod. 
*   **Power:** You’ll need a **Manual Generator** (hamster wheel) and a **Tiny Battery** (under the **Power** tab) connected with wires to make it run.

### 4. Sleeping
*   **What to build:** In the **Furniture** tab, build **3 Cots** (one for each Duplicant).
*   **Where:** Place them together in a room to create a "Barracks," which gives them a morale boost.

### 5. Find Liquid Water
Looking at your screenshot, that big blue area below you is mostly **Ice**. You can't pump ice!
*   **Where to look:** Look at the first image, the green area at the very top has a pool of **Liquid Water**. 
*   **Action:** Dig a path upward toward that green biome. Once you reach the water, build a **Pitcher Pump** (Plumbing tab) over it. Your Duplicants need this liquid water to fill the Wash Basin so they stay clean.

### 6. Food (The Cold Problem)
You start with some rations (16,000 kcal), but you’ll need more.
*   **Short term:** Dig into any tiles that have "cracks" in them (Buried Objects). These often contain **Muckroot**, which is free food.
*   **Long term:** Since you are in a cold area, normal plants like Mealwood might not grow because it's too chilly. Look for **Hexalent** fruit in the surroundings for now while you research heaters.

**Summary of your first dig path:**
1.  Dig **Right** for a Bathroom.
2.  Dig **Left** for a Bedroom.
3.  Dig **Up** to reach that liquid water pool in the green area.

Good luck, and try not to let them hold it in for too long!
