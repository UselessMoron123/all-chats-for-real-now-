### 🌍 Setting: The Cubical Fantasy World
The land is literally cubical. Every hill is stair‑stepped, every cloud a hovering cube of mist, and every villager’s house looks like someone built it in a voxel editor after too much coffee. Magic is common, but efficiency is not: half the wizards here spend their time enchanting teapots to gossip.

---

### 🧍‍♂️You, the Unemployed Villager
**Name:** Optional, but villagers currently refer to you as **“That One Guy Who’s Always on Break.”**  
**Class:** None officially recognized. Local registry lists your occupation as *“potentially useful if motivated.”*  
**Stats (approximate):**  
- HP: 8 (drops to 6 after existential crises)  
- MP: 11 (used exclusively for heating leftovers)  
- Charisma: 9 (you can charm pigeons into giving moral support)  
- Luck: fluctuates wildly depending on snacks available

### ☀️ Scene 1: Another Gloriously Ordinary Morning
The square sun has risen precisely at a 90‑degree angle. Roosters crow in perfect cube notes. You awaken in your modest 3x3x3 hut to the smell of… slightly burnt bread. The Bread Golem next door has been stress‑baking again.

Your quest log (written on suspiciously sticky parchment) reads:
1. Find a job.  
2. Maybe.  
3. If there’s coffee first.

A villager knocks on your door — it’s Mira, the overly dedicated Guild Intern. She’s balancing nine clipboards and a sense of overachievement.  

**Mira:** *“Good morning, uh… you! The Guild said they can no longer process your Unemployment XP unless you take *some* quest before noon. Apparently, loitering doesn’t count as exploration.”*

---

How would your profoundly unambitious alter ego respond to this sudden burst of responsibility?  

1. **Feign heroism** — “Of course! I was *just* about to embark on a brave job hunt… after breakfast.”  
2. **Negotiate bureaucracy** — Attempt to convince Mira that being unemployed is critical world‑building research.  
3. **Panic‑nap** — Collapse into your cube‑bed and try to sleep the problem away.  
4. **Something completely unexpected** — unleash your true “talent,” whatever that might be.
