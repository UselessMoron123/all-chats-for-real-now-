# 🏔 THE AZURE CLOUD SAGA
### Chapter One — The Day of Testing

Half a month ago, you died.

You remember rain on a crosswalk. A phone glowing at 2 a.m. Headlights. A horn. And one strangely calm thought: *at least I finished the noodles.*

Then — fever. A rough blanket smelling of woodsmoke and turnips. And a second set of memories blooming in your skull like ink dropped in water: fourteen years of them. Harvests. A mother's lullaby in a language you now dream in. A war with the village geese, ongoing since you were five, with scars on both sides.

You are — you were — now you are again: **Li Shitou**, "Little Stone," fourteen, of Green Ox Village. Grandmother named you Stone so the ghosts would think you nothing worth taking. It worked on the ghosts. *Not on the geese.*

This morning, your father woke you in the dark.

"Get up," said Li Daniu — a man with a back like a barn door and hands like tree roots — and his voice shook like the time the ox got into the wine. "The immortals come to Willow County today."

---

The **Azure Cloud Sect** opens its gates once every three years: every child aged eight to sixteen may be tested for a **spirit root**. You're fourteen — the next testing would find you past the cutoff, your bones "set like clay out of the kiln," as the teacher says.

*Last chance.* For your sister Xiaoyu — eight, braids, a gap where a tooth used to be — first one.

The county temple courtyard is packed with farm families and one very bored man in white robes: **Deacon Zhao**, beside a crystal orb the size of a melon. Leaning on a sword nearby, a young woman watches the crowd the way a cat watches a river. The villagers bow to her and whisper: *the fairy.*

"Listen well," Zhao drones. "Qi Refining opens the body's twelve meridians. Foundation Establishment lets you walk on air. Core Formation elders live three hundred years. If the orb lights for you, the mountain feeds you, and even the lowest outer disciple eats spirit rice. Now. Line up. Touch the orb. Don't *drop* anything."

Wang Fugui, the miller's son, goes first in silk robes. The orb glows a muddy yellow. "Earth root, lower grade," Zhao sighs. Fugui's father announces to the entire county that this is *hidden genius*.

Then Xiaoyu. Her small palm touches the crystal—

Light. Clear blue, bright as spring water, *rising*.

Deacon Zhao sits up straight for the first time all day. "Water spirit root. **Upper purity.**" Gasps. Murmurs. A jade token pressed into eight small hands. "The child departs with us in three days. Understand this, all of you — from that day, *the mountain does not answer to parents.*"

Your mother begins to cry. Your father bows so low his forehead gathers dust.

Then it's your turn.

Your palm settles on the orb, cool as river stone. You wait. Nothing. Inside the crystal — fog. Gray, like breath on glass.

But for three heartbeats — *the world tears open.*

**You SEE.** Threads of light everywhere, alive, flowing: every child in the courtyard glows faintly, like coals under ash. Xiaoyu is a lantern. The fairy is a bonfire wearing skin. The orb *drinks* light from the sky itself. And you look down at yourself and find — one thread. Gray. Running from your chest *straight down*, like a root, deep into the earth.

And something blooms in your mind, ink in water, shaped like words:

> 【**Anomaly recorded.**】

Then it's gone. The orb shows the same gray fog. Deacon Zhao taps the crystal twice, re-tests a girl behind you (her orb glows fine), frowns at you once more.

"Mortal frame. No root. **Next.**"

Somewhere behind you, Fugui's father becomes very loudly sympathetic.

---

The ox cart creaks home through the dusk. Xiaoyu tries, twice, to secretly give the jade token back — *"I don't want to go if ge-ge can't come"* — and your mother presses warm eggs into *your* hands, not hers. Your father is silent for three li of road. Then: "There are fields that need a strong back, Stone." And nothing more.

These people were strangers in your head two weeks ago. Now the thought of Xiaoyu on that mountain, alone, among people who *do not answer to parents* — it makes your chest hurt in a way the old world never managed.

That night, on the straw mattress, moonlight on the wall, you reach inward on some new instinct—

—and **gray pages open.**

```
【 THE GRAY PAGES · folio one of ——— 】

 Bearer:    Li Shitou ("Little Stone"), 14, mortal
 Realm:     Qi Refining, Stage 0 — unopened
 Meridians: 12 of 12 sealed
 Qi:        none sensed
 Spirit Root: [ the ink pools here, and will not dry ]
 Techniques: none
 Deeds:     —

 ~ a note in the margin, in handwriting not your own ~
   "The stone the builders discard
    becomes the cornerstone."
```

The pages wait. Somewhere in the dark, a goose honks, as if it knows.

### ⚔ What do you do?

The sect leaves with Xiaoyu in **three days.**

**1. Swallow your pride.** Walk to the county seat, kneel before Deacon Zhao, and beg for *any* place in the Azure Cloud Sect — kitchen, stables, sweeping. Where your sister goes, you go. *(Path of the Sect Grunt)*

**2. Let her fly.** Stay in the village. Fields by day — but by night, follow the gray pages wherever they lead. No one in this world's ten thousand years has ever cultivated like you're about to. *(Path of the Heretic Farmer)*

**3. Beg Xiaoyu to refuse the token.** A sect is a den of strangers with swords. Family is family. *(Cost: her destiny — and maybe her resentment)*

**4. Anything else.** Speak or act freely — the world answers. *(Interrogate father about his own past? Spy on the strange thread in your chest? Sneak to the county town alone? Your call.)*
