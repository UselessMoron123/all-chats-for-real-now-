At the roadside, a hand-painted sign has been nailed to a post:

**“BY ORDER OF LORD FARQUAAD — FAIRYTALE CREATURES MUST BE SURRENDERED. REWARD PAID.”**

Two knights in polished helmets stop a family on the road. The “family” is trying very hard to look normal: a hunched old woman with a basket, a skinny man sweating through his tunic, and a “baby” bundled so tightly you can’t even see its face.

The knight pokes the bundle with the tip of his spear.

“Babies don’t hiss,” he says.

From inside the cloth comes a small, unmistakable *ssssss*.

The skinny man laughs too loudly. “Allergies! Seasonal. Very common—”

The second knight grabs the basket. It’s full of turnips… and one tiny glass slipper.

His voice hardens. “That’s contraband.”

The old woman’s eyes flick up to you, sharp and pleading, like she’s measuring whether you’re the kind of person who helps… or the kind who takes the reward.

Around you, a few travelers slow down to watch. Nobody steps in.

You are close enough to smell the knights’ leather and the dust kicked up by the wagons. Close enough to act before this turns into chains and a long ride to Duloc.

What do you do?

- **Step in boldly**: talk your way between the knights and the “family.”
- **Cause a distraction**: knock over a cart, spook a horse, anything to break the moment.
- **Play the bounty angle**: pretend you’re helping the knights, then twist it.
- **Stay hidden**: follow the wagons quietly and see where they’re taking everyone.
