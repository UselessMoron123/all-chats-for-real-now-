- He was a **major Norman ruler**

- he was more of an **adventurer-conqueror**
- his power base was less stable than Roger’s
- Antioch was prestigious, but also **far away**

- Venice’s interests were often commercial and maritime, not focused on strengthening Matilda’s territorial rule
- a marriage to a doge would be a much less natural feudal-dynastic match

Also, Venetian political culture was not especially centered on marriage alliances in the same way as princely houses.
