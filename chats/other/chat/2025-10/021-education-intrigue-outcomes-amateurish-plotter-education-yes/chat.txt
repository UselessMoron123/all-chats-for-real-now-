    *   **`Master Schemer`:** The lifestyle choice that shows his dedication to the craft.

**Why it's Universal:** By swapping `Cynical` for `Patient`, his personality becomes less grating. He now appears as a quiet, thoughtful, and hyper-aware guardian. A nomad Khan respects a vigilant watchman, and a feudal King respects a spymaster who stops plots with quiet efficiency. The core danger is still there, but it is masked by a more professional and less overtly negative demeanor.

### **5. The Spiritual / Physician: The Wise Sage**

**Concept:** This build is already **perfectly universal**. The pursuit of knowledge, science, and health is respected in every culture. A man who can heal the king's son or provide wise counsel is an asset everywhere.

*   **Education:** `Mastermind Theologian`
*   **Universal Core:** `Genius`, `Ambitious`, `Shrewd`, `Diligent`
*   **Specialist & Personality Traits:**
    *   **`Physician`:** A life-saver. Its value is absolute.
    *   **`Scholar`:** Signals a life dedicated to learning.
    *   **`Patient`:** Reinforces the image of a wise, thoughtful man.
    *   **`Erudite`:** A respected intellectual.

**Why it's Universal:** There is no culture in the game that disrespects a brilliant doctor and scholar. This character is the easiest to slot into any court and have him be immediately revered for his immense knowledge and utility.
