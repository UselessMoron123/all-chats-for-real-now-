To create a "Universal Trio" that can survive anywhere from the ice sheet to the jungle without constant rerolling, you need to cover **all critical survival pillars** across three pawns.

In RimWorld, **Passions (the flame icons)** are actually more important than the starting number. A pawn with level 3 and a double flame is better than a level 8 with no flame.

Here is the "Gold Middle" template for your three starters:

---

### Pawn 1: The Architect (Construction & Mining)
*This pawn is responsible for your physical base. Without them, you can't build coolers, heaters, or power grids.*

*   **Primary Skill: Construction (6-8+).** Essential for building beds and power generators without failing and wasting materials.
*   **Secondary Skill: Mining (5+).** Necessary for expanding into mountains and gathering steel/components early.
*   **Combat: Shooting.** Usually your primary defender.
*   **Critical "Must-Have":** Must be capable of **Hauling and Cleaning**.

### Pawn 2: The Provider (Plants, Cooking, & Medicine)
*This is your survival specialist. They keep the colony fed and stop people from dying of infections.*

*   **Primary Skill: Plants (8+).** This is the magic number because it allows you to harvest **Healroot** (herbal medicine) immediately.
*   **Secondary Skill: Medicine (6-8+).** You need one person who can reliably patch up wounds and perform surgery.
*   **Secondary Skill: Cooking (4-6+).** At level 4, the "Food Poisoning" chance drops significantly.
*   **Combat: Shooting or Melee.** 

### Pawn 3: The Brains (Intellectual, Social, & Crafting)
*This pawn ensures you don't get stuck in the Stone Age and handles the "outside world."*

*   **Primary Skill: Intellectual (6-8+).** Research is the only way to progress. In a "starve or die" situation, getting to Hydroponics or Microelectronics quickly is key.
*   **Secondary Skill: Social (6+).** For recruiting prisoners and getting better prices from traders.
*   **Secondary Skill: Crafting (4+).** Useful for making basic clothes and weapons early on.
*   **Combat: Shooting.** 

---

### The "Universal Check-List" (Before you hit Start)

Before you embark, check the **Left Side** of the character selection screen. Ensure these three things are true:

1.  **The "No-Go" List:** None of the three pawns should have "None" or "Disabled" for:
    *   **Dumb Labor** (Everyone must be able to haul and clean).
    *   **Violence** (Everyone must be able to fight).
    *   **Caring** (Everyone should be able to stabilize a wound in an emergency).
2.  **The "Healroot" Check:** At least one pawn must have **Plants 8**. If you land in a biome with a short growing season and you can't grow your own medicine, you will likely lose to the first infection you get.
3.  **The Overlap:** Make sure you have at least **two** people with a passion in **Medicine**. If your only doctor gets downed in a raid, your colony is effectively dead. 

### Summary for "The Gold Middle"
*   **Pawn 1:** High **Construction**, decent Mining.
*   **Pawn 2:** High **Plants**, decent Medicine/Cooking.
*   **Pawn 3:** High **Intellectual**, decent Social/Crafting.

If you have this spread, you can land in a Desert, a Tundra, or a Forest and have the tools to survive the first year every time.
